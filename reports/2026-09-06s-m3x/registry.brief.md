## Reading

Three wings hold this repository, and the marks do not sit evenly in them. The scripts wing carries 8 of the 11 import_root rooms; it also carries 1 of the 27 dark_room rooms — a long-untouched room — and 1 of the 26 flooded_basement rooms — a long-untouched, still-imported room [import_root ×11: scripts/backfill-references.ts, scripts/calibration-gate.ts; dark_room ×27: scripts/run-seed.ts; flooded_basement ×26: scripts/run-seed.ts]. The import_root predicate reads fan_in and fan_out and no declaration of entry, so a package entry that other rooms import cannot qualify and one that nothing imports can; that is a limit on the reading, not a description of the rooms.

The wide marks sit in the same neighbourhoods of the tree. scaffolding — a test-imported room at or above the median reinforcement, a position, not a claim about support — fires on 133 rooms, and lit_room on 40 [scaffolding ×133: src/services/safety/decode.ts, src/utils/circuit-breaker.ts; lit_room ×40: src/controllers/user-controller.ts]. The narrower positions name hubs and ends: foundation — a high-load hub — on 27, hub on 27, leaf_utility on 20, corridor on 13, and package_entry on one room [foundation ×27: src/db/connection.ts; hub ×27: src/schemas/fork/schema.ts; leaf_utility ×20: src/utils/json.ts; corridor ×13: src/config/database.ts; package_entry ×1: src/index.ts].

The register draws 4 relations, 1 identical and 3 within. The two foundation marks, one under each profile, name the same 27 rooms under the same predicate, and 27 rooms carry two marks for that reason alone [foundation ×27]. flooded_basement nests inside dark_room, its extra conjunct removing 1 room; corridor nests inside hub with 14 rooms outside it, and inside scaffolding with 120 outside [corridor ×13]. So the 11 diagnostic features name 10 distinct sets of rooms, and 88 rooms carry two or more diagnostic marks.

27 decorative marks render but are not a diagnosis: crack — a high edit-pressure node — rests on bug_pressure_index, which is unvalidated [crack ×27].

The diagnosis presupposes a maintenance norm — that the positions it marks are worth a visit — which the reader may reject; it is stated as an ought, not a fact.
