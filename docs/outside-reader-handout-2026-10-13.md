# Reader exercise — participant handout

Please use the two supplied artifacts in order. This is an exercise in reading the
artifacts, not a test of your programming knowledge. You may say that you cannot tell.

## Part A — brief (about one minute)

Read the brief as you would if a colleague sent it before a maintenance meeting.
Then answer, in your own words:

1. What would you tell that meeting about this repository? Give up to three observations.
2. What, if anything, would you investigate next? What led you to that choice?
3. How confident are you in those observations, and what questions remain?

## Part B — change sheet (two to three minutes)

Explore the supplied change sheet as you normally would. Then answer:

1. What changed between the two snapshots?
2. What, if anything, does that suggest doing next?
3. What can you tell about why the displayed changes happened? What remains unclear?

After recording both parts, point to the parts of the artifacts that supported your
answers. Would you revise any of your earlier observations? Record any revision
separately so the initial answer remains visible.
